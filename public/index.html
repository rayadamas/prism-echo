YOUR FIRST COMPOSITION

A little separation.
A lot of possibility.

  1. Start with an image. A simple background or transparent cutout gives the cleanest subject edges.
  2. Explore. Try a composition and palette, then hit New variation. Separation and Fragmentation change how the echoes move.
  3. Keep yourself present. Set face focus on your face and adjust protection. Refine the subject mask when needed.
  4. Take it with you. Download the finished image or just the transparent color overlay. Save a recipe to repeat the result with the same source image.

The studio builds variations from the pixels you provide; it preserves your photograph rather than redrawing it. Images are processed locally in your browser.
